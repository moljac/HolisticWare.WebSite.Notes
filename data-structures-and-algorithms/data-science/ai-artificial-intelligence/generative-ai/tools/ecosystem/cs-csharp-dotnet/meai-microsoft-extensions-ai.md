*   Apple Intelligence

    Apple Intelligence uses a hybrid model, meaning it runs partially local on your device and partially in the cloud.How the Local Part WorksOn-Device Models: Apple uses a lightweight on-device foundation model (around 3 billion parameters) to handle simpler, everyday tasks directly on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.Local Tasks: Features like writing tools (proofreading and rewriting), quick notification summaries, and basic local app searches run entirely on your hardware without sending data away.Privacy: Keeping these tasks local ensures your personal data stays on your device and is not collected by Apple.How the Cloud Part WorksPrivate Cloud Compute: When a request is too complex for the small on-device model, Apple Intelligence routes it to Private Cloud Compute, which uses larger server-based models.Cloud Privacy: Apple states that your data is encrypted, processed securely on dedicated Apple silicon servers (or partner server setups), and never stored or made accessible to Apple.
